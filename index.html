Why this project matters

Helping young people see a future in their craft.

CraftStep Digital shows students that shoemaking is not an outdated occupation, but a real European profession built on skill, technology and market value.

Through the development of 15 prototypes, industrial finalisation and digital traceability, the project connects school-based learning with real production and market environments. Students follow how an idea becomes a product, how a product gains identity, and how professional skills can open a path to employment and personal confidence.

Traditional craft

Learners build practical skills through tangible shoemaking results.

Digital identity

Each product receives an NFC-based profile and documented production pathway.

Sustainable thinking

The model promotes durable products, resource awareness and solar-powered visibility.

From classroom to market

Students see how their work can be presented, valued and connected to real opportunities.

CraftStep Digital is not only about making shoes. It is about helping students discover pride in their profession, confidence in their skills and motivation to build their future.

Work packages

Four phases that turn a student idea into a visible product.

Each work package follows the Craft → Industry → Market logic and gives students a clear step in the product journey: from concept and materials, through industrial production, to market presentation and public review.

WP1
15 Oct 2026 – 15 Jan 2027Bulgaria · Peshtera and SofiaLead: BORDO 15

CRAFT Phase: Concept Design and Technical Preparation

This phase starts the vertically integrated Craft–Industry–Market model. Students work on footwear categories, individual concepts, technical sketches, materials and sustainable production logic. The goal is to turn their first idea into a structured technical production brief that can continue into the industrial phase.

Main activities

  • Concept development and initial sketches
  • Guided work with a master craftsman and designer
  • Material selection, quality criteria and zero-waste cutting principles
  • Introduction to NFC and digital product identity

Expected result

15 Technical Production Files with specifications, materials, last selection and digital traceability entry point.

Students learn that a shoe begins with an idea, but becomes real through structure, materials and professional preparation.
WP2
16 Jan 2027 – 15 Apr 2027North MacedoniaLead: GLECHER DOOEL

INDUSTRY Phase: Industrial Footwear Production and Components Pathway

This phase moves the student models into a real manufacturing environment. Learners observe and analyse component preparation, cutting, stitching, assembly and quality control. The technical files from WP1 become industrially finalised prototypes, while NFC components connect production with digital traceability.

Main activities

  • Industrial production of the 15 student models
  • Visit to a footwear components factory
  • Physical NFC integration into the production pathway
  • Updating digital product profiles and mid-phase assessment

Expected result

15 industrially finalised prototypes with activated digital profiles documenting the full production pathway.

Students see how design decisions, technical documentation and factory workflow become a finished product.
WP3
16 Apr 2027 – 17 Jul 2027GreeceLead: KANABOY SOFIA

MARKET Phase: Design and Digital Market Integration

This phase completes the product as a market-oriented object. Students refine finishing, functionality and presentation, then connect each prototype with product identity, positioning logic, visual consistency and digital product profiles. The focus is not commercial sales, but understanding how a handcrafted product can be communicated and valued.

Main activities

  • Branding guidance and visual identity development
  • Pricing logic and product positioning exercises
  • Market-oriented product presentation
  • Completion of full digital product profiles

Expected result

15 refined prototypes with consolidated product identity and market-ready digital presentation.

Students understand that professional value also depends on presentation, identity and the ability to explain a product.
WP4
18 Jul 2027 – 14 Oct 2027Bulgaria · PeshteraLead: Vivonda

Digital Showcase and EQAVET Stakeholder Review

The final phase presents the completed Craft–Industry–Market journey to the school community, local sector and external stakeholders. A permanent Solar-powered Digital Showcase is installed at the school. Each product connects to its NFC and QR-accessible profile, while students present the logic behind their design, production choices and market relevance.

Main activities

  • Installation and testing of the solar-powered digital display
  • Presentation of the 15 completed products
  • Verification of all QR and NFC digital profiles
  • Stakeholder feedback, final assessment and EQAVET Review Meeting

Expected result

A public presentation event, structured external review, stakeholder feedback and documented improvement measures.

The project ends with visibility, evidence and review, not with a closed classroom activity.

CraftStep Digital gives 15 VET learners the opportunity to experience the complete product development cycle: from the first sketch, through technical preparation and industrial production, to digital identity and public presentation.

Students begin by transforming their own ideas into original footwear concepts. Through sketches and guided design work, creativity becomes the starting point of every product.
