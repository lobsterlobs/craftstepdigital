25 September 2026 · Digital platform

The official CraftStep Digital platform is now online.

craftstepdigital.eu is the official public platform of CraftStep Digital: Learning Shoemaking Through Real Practice. It will document the project throughout its implementation and bring together the partnership, activities and results in one accessible digital space.

The platform follows the full Craft → Industry → Market pathway: practical craft learning in Bulgaria, industrial footwear production in North Macedonia, and digital market presentation in Greece. Visitors will find information about the project objectives, work packages, partners, timeline and EQAVET quality approach.

As the project develops, the platform will grow with news from meetings and practical sessions, student work, prototypes, NFC-based digital product profiles and public project results. Its purpose is not only to present information, but to create a visible digital record of how a student idea becomes a real, traceable product.

26 September 2026 · Project preparation

Preparing for our first CraftStep Digital project meeting.

The partnership is preparing for the first project meeting and the practical start of the joint work. This first step will bring the partners together around the implementation plan, responsibilities and the opening phase of the Craft → Industry → Market learning pathway.

As part of the preparation, the official CraftStep Digital visual materials are ready. The two project roll-ups present the journey of one shoe across three countries and the link between student ideas, real practice, QR/NFC connectivity and digital product identity.

The roll-ups will accompany partner meetings, learning activities and public presentations in Bulgaria, North Macedonia and Greece. We are looking forward to bringing the team together and starting the first practical activities.
